How I Work Through Problems

I try to keep the process simple: understand the real problem, build something concrete, then improve it with evidence.

Understand & Define

Clarify the problem, constraints, users and what a useful outcome should actually look like.

Build & Test

Turn the idea into a working version, validate the important parts and keep the implementation understandable.

Iterate & Improve

Use feedback, tests and real usage to improve the product instead of polishing assumptions.
