WRITING

Writing helps me understand things better.

I use this space to work through ideas, explain what I’m learning and share thoughts on topics I find interesting. The idea for this blog was inspired by a lecture by Juha-Matti Santala, who spoke about the value of writing as a way to structure your thinking and learn to explain complex ideas more clearly — both to yourself and to others. Most posts will revolve around software, data, AI and technology, but not necessarily all of them.
